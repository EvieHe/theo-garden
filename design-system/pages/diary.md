# Diary Page

Extends `../MASTER.md`.

## Purpose
A visual calendar that lets dates with memories become image portals. Empty dates remain simple numerals. Clicking a memory should feel like entering that day, not opening a record.

## Calendar composition
- No traditional calendar grid lines.
- Preserve recognizable 7-column calendar rhythm.
- Memory days are circular photographic nodes.
- Empty days are bare date numerals.
- Keep generous negative space around the calendar.
- Background should be atmospheric photography with a cool-to-warm translucent wash, inspired by editorial campaign layouts rather than productivity apps.

## Day story composition
- Hero: oversized date layered over a single dominant photograph.
- Story: alternate image/text editorial spreads with large vertical breathing room.
- End: next-memory navigation as a quiet editorial close.

## Motion moments
1. Calendar entrance: month copy, glass calendar, then date cells stagger in.
2. Pointer: subtle localized light and very small background parallax.
3. Memory hover: lift, scale, glass highlight shift, image zoom.
4. Memory click: circular node expands toward story-hero proportions before navigation.
5. Day hero: image and date move at different scroll speeds.
6. Story blocks: reveal once on intersection; image settles a few pixels after text.

## Glass usage
Allowed: calendar surface, month switcher, story nav, tiny date badge.
Avoid: wrapping diary text or each story image in glass cards.
